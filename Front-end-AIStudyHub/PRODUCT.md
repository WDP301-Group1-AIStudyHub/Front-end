# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and instructors who organize study documents, share learning resources, generate study materials, and use AI chat to work with document content. They often return frequently, scan dense lists, and need to find or act on a document quickly.

## Product Purpose

AI Study Hub provides one trusted workspace for managing academic documents and turning them into useful study activity. Success means users can upload, organize, share, retrieve, study, and discuss documents without losing context or confidence in permissions and system state.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and focused. The interface should feel calm and capable, with familiar document-management patterns and concise feedback.

## Anti-references

Avoid pale blue-green page washes, botanical decoration, marketing-style dashboard composition, excessive cards, oversized rounded surfaces, low-contrast text, decorative gradients, and unfamiliar controls. The product should not feel whimsical, ornamental, or visually submerged.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the document and the next useful action visually obvious.
2. Prefer familiar management patterns over decorative novelty.
3. Show permissions, ownership, processing, and destructive states clearly.
4. Reuse one interaction vocabulary across user and admin surfaces.
5. Make dense information easy to scan without making the interface feel crowded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation across all primary workflows. Support visible focus, reduced motion, screen-reader labels, color-independent status cues, responsive layouts, and robust text wrapping for Vietnamese and English content.
